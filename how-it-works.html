Illustrative

A note leaves. A payment arrives.

This is the planned path of a private payment. The animation is a diagram. It is not a tracker, not a mempool, and not your funds.

How it works — an illustrative sequence of the planned flow. Not a live transaction, and not your funds.

Veil

You would deposit ETH, USDC, or USDG. The browser builds an encrypted note and keeps the spend secret with you. The pool would record a commitment, not your identity.

Prove

The browser prepares a zero-knowledge proof that a note is yours and unspent, without revealing which note. The proving system is not chosen yet. This page does not generate a proof.

Relay

A relayer is planned to submit the proof and pay the network fee, so your own wallet is not the sender. The relayer would see the withdrawal it is asked to broadcast.

Arrive

The recipient address is paid. Observers are meant to see an arrival, not a public link back to the deposit. That is the design goal. It is not a live payment.

Read it slowly

Veil creates the note. Prove shows the note is spendable without pointing at it. Relay puts that proof on a chain under someone else’s gas. Arrive is the recipient being paid. The link the pool is designed to withhold is the one between the first step and the last.

What this diagram is not

  • Not a live payment feed.
  • Not evidence of volume, users, or deposits.
  • Not a claim that privacy is absolute. A relayer sees what it submits. A recipient sees what arrives. Amounts and timing remain visible as amounts and timing.
Nothing in the sequence can be submitted. Contracts are not deployed. The app’s buttons stay on “Launching soon”.
