The path of a payment, from first shadow to arrival.

Veil

A note is born. ETH, USDC, or USDG, held as a secret only its owner can spend.

Prove

The browser draws a proof of that note, and leaves the name of it unspoken.

Relay

Another hand pays the gas. The chain records that hand, not yours.

Arrive

Value reaches an address. The road back stays dark.

Read it by moonlight

Veil makes the note. Prove shows it can be spent without pointing at it. Relay lays that proof on a chain under someone else’s gas. Arrive is an address, paid. The dark is the missing line between the first and the last.
