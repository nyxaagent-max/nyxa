Is Nyxa Network live?

This website is phase zero. Testnet pools and a relayer are phase one. The order is on the roadmap.

What does the app do today?

It connects your wallet and reads the address, the network, and the native balance. The rites wait. The button reads Launching soon.

Where is the $NYXA address?

0xfabf7473d2847e7a8c650851b64737d15505cf0c on Robinhood Chain. The contract name is NYXA NETWORK and the symbol is NYXA.

Has there been an audit?

Audit is phase two. It has not begun. Nyxa does not name a reviewer, a report, or a date.

Which assets?

ETH, USDC, and USDG. The balance in the app is the chain’s own gas token, read from your wallet.

Which chains?

Robinhood Chain, Base, Arbitrum, and BNB Chain for the pool. Solana as a destination. Details live on Network.

Who holds the secret?

You do. Nyxa is built to never keep it. Dawn is a way back through your own wallet, not a spare kept by anyone else.

What does a relayer see?

The payment it agrees to carry, including the destination. Others are meant to see the arrival, not which note it came from.

Does the dark hide everything?

No. Recipients see what arrives. Relayers see what they send. Amounts and timing remain. The missing piece is the public link between a deposit and a later payment.

Is there an official social account?

Yes. @nyxanetwork is linked in the footer, next to GitHub.

Someone sent me a contract address.

Match it to 0xfabf7473d2847e7a8c650851b64737d15505cf0c on Robinhood Chain. Any other address is not $NYXA.
