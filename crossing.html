Nyxa Network / Crossing

In development

Cross without a public trail.

Crossing is planned to move value between Robinhood Chain, Base, Arbitrum, and BNB Chain, and onward to Solana as a destination.

Cross-chain movement adds a hop between networks. The design goal is that the source deposit and the destination payment stay unlinkable on public explorers. The mechanism is not frozen, and nothing is deployed.

Nyxa will not ask you to send funds to a personal “bridge address” on this website. There is no deposit address to paste.

Roles, as planned

Source

An EVM network where the note was veiled: Robinhood Chain, Base, Arbitrum, or BNB Chain.

Destination

One of those EVM networks, or Solana. Solana is not a network you connect in the EVM wallet switcher.

Status

Not connected to Nyxa contracts, because those contracts do not exist yet.
