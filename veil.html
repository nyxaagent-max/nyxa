Nyxa Network / Veil

In development

Shield a note.

Veil is the deposit. ETH, USDC, or USDG would enter the pool as an encrypted note that only the holder can spend. Nothing on this page accepts a deposit.

A note is the private record of a deposit: a secret you hold, and a commitment the pool can store without publishing who you are. Spending it later is Whisper, Eclipse, or Crossing. Reconstructing it from the same wallet is Dawn.

The planned gesture

  1. 01

    Choose

    Pick the asset and the amount. The planned assets are ETH, USDC, and USDG. No pool balance exists to display.

  2. 02

    Commit

    The browser would build a secret and a commitment. The secret stays with you. The commitment is what the pool would record.

  3. 03

    Wait

    Until contracts exist, there is nowhere to submit that commitment. The app lets you fill in the form and then stops.

Diagram, not a receipt. Nyxa cannot create a note today. Any page or message that offers a deposit address is not this project.
