Veil, prove, relay, arrive.

The sequence plays on its own. It is a diagram of the plan.

How it works — an illustrative sequence of the planned flow. Not a live transaction, and not your funds.

Veil

You would deposit ETH, USDC, or USDG. The browser builds an encrypted note and keeps the spend secret with you. The pool would record a commitment, not your identity.

Prove

The browser prepares a zero-knowledge proof that a note is yours and unspent, without revealing which note. The proving system is not chosen yet. This page does not generate a proof.

Relay

A relayer is planned to submit the proof and pay the network fee, so your own wallet is not the sender. The relayer would see the withdrawal it is asked to broadcast.

Arrive

The recipient address is paid. Observers are meant to see an arrival, not a public link back to the deposit. That is the design goal. It is not a live payment.
