Questions, in a low voice

Where is the proof made?

In the browser, beside the note. A relayer is meant to receive a finished proof, not the secret.

Which chains?

Robinhood Chain, Base, Arbitrum, and BNB Chain for the pool. Solana and Ethereum are far shores for Crossing, not homes for the pool.

Where is the $NYXA address?

It will be announced only on official channels. Until then the token page stays quiet.

What is the terminal showing?

Public chain data: block numbers, gas, and recent transaction hashes from Robinhood Chain, Base, Arbitrum, and BNB Chain. Those are not Nyxa payments.

Has there been an audit?

Audit is phase two of the roadmap. It has not begun.
